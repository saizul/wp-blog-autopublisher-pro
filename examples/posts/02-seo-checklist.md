## Why a checklist

Consistency beats heroics. A checklist catches the boring mistakes.

| Check | What to look for |
|-------|------------------|
| Intent | Does the post answer the query it targets? |
| Title | Under about 60 characters, keyword near the front |
| Links | At least two relevant internal links |
